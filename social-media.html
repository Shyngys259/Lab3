Social Media

Social Media

Social media can help us communicate and share information, but mindful use is important.

The feed is designed to be endless, and it is very good at it. There is no natural stopping point — no credits, no last page, nothing that tells you the experience is finished. That is not a flaw in your character, it is a feature of the product. Mindful use therefore means adding the stopping point yourself, before you open the app rather than after the third hour.

Better Social Media Habits

  • Take regular social media breaks.
  • Avoid endless scrolling.
  • Follow useful and positive content.
  • Spend time with people offline.

A Quick Audit of Your Feed

+

Keep

Accounts that teach you something, make you laugh honestly, or keep you genuinely close to people you actually know.

~

Limit

Accounts you enjoy but that leave you comparing your life to theirs. Muted, not unfollowed — they stay reachable when you want them.

−

Remove

Anything that reliably makes you feel worse: outrage loops, appearance comparison, and people you would not choose to spend an afternoon with.

The two-minute reset

Once a month, open your following list and read it top to bottom. Unfollow or mute anything that does not pass the audit. It takes two minutes and quietly changes what you see every day afterwards.
