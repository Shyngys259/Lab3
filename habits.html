Digital Detox & Reset

Sometimes the habits have slipped far enough that tinkering is not enough, and a short deliberate break is the faster route back. A reset is not about proving you can live without your phone. It is about remembering what the unoccupied hours feel like, so you can choose what to put back in them.

A 24-Hour Reset

  • Tell people you will be slow to reply.
  • Pick the day in advance — a weekend works better.
  • Plan two things to do instead, in writing.
  • Keep calls available; remove the feed.

A 7-Day Reset

  • Delete the three heaviest apps from your phone.
  • Use them only in a browser, at a desk, once a day.
  • Keep a note of what you missed and what you did not.
  • Reinstall on day eight only what earned its place.
Expect the first day to feel bad

Restlessness, phantom vibrations and an urge to reach for a pocket that is empty are normal and they pass. What usually arrives on day two is the thing worth having: a long, quiet stretch of time that is entirely yours.
